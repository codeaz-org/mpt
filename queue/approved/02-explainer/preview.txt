--- SCRIPT (114 words) ---
Google login works in the preview but fails on your live site. You see a redirect error that says redirect_uri_mismatch. That happens because Google will only send users back to URLs you have explicitly registered. In the builder’s preview, that URL is automatically added, but your real domain isn’t on the list, so Google rejects the request. Fix it by adding your production callback URL to your Google Cloud project’s Authorized Redirect URLs. Then add the same URL in Supabase Auth’s redirect settings. Finally, test the login on the live domain before you launch. If you’d rather not do it alone, get a written audit of your app at rescue dot codeaz dot org.
